# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Book Pet welcomes people of every background and experience level. We are
committed to a friendly, safe, and inclusive community where contributors can
learn, experiment, and collaborate without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome beginners and explain unfamiliar concepts patiently.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and differing perspectives.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Encouraging unsafe device use while presenting it as verified
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
For security-sensitive technical reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with respect for the privacy and safety
of everyone involved.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
